Cashless

  • Get admitted at a network hospital
  • Notify us within 24 hours of admission
  • Our team coordinates directly with the hospital

Reimbursement

Treated at a non-network hospital or paid out of pocket

  • Pay bills yourself at the time of treatment
  • Submit documents within 15 days of discharge
  • Eligible amount transferred to your bank account
File Reimbursement Claim

Own Damage

Accident, fire, flood, or natural calamity

  • Inform us immediately after the incident
  • Don't move vehicle before inspection if possible
  • Cashless repairs at network garages available

  • File an FIR immediately
  • Inform us within 24 hours

Death Claim
Term & Life

Filed by the nominee on the death of the life assured

  • Intimate us as early as possible with the policy number
  • Submit the death certificate and nominee KYC
  • For unnatural death, FIR & post-mortem report are required
  • Sum assured paid directly to the registered nominee
Who Is My Nominee? Intimate a Death Claim

Terminal & Critical Illness
Accelerated

An advance payout while the life assured is still living

  • Available if your plan includes a terminal/critical illness rider
  • Submit a diagnosis certified by a registered specialist
  • Benefit is paid to the policyholder, subject to plan terms
Check Rider Eligibility File an Illness Claim

Maturity & Survival Benefit

For endowment, savings & money-back plans on maturity

  • We notify you before the maturity date — no rush
  • Submit the discharge form, policy bond and bank details
  • Maturity amount credited to your registered bank account
Claim Maturity Benefit

Structure Damage

  • Document damage with photos and videos immediately

  • File within 7 days of the incident

What We Do Once You've Told Us

Here's who's doing what at every stage — and where we step in.

Claim Registered

We intimate your insurer and send you a claim number by email and SMS, so there's a record from day one.

Within 24 hrs

Document Check

We review your documents against your insurer's requirement list and come back to you on anything missing — before it reaches them.

2–3 working days

Filed & Tracked

Your file goes to the insurer's assessor. We follow up, respond to their queries, and keep you posted so you're never left guessing.

5–7 working days

Insurer's Decision

Approval, partial approval, or rejection. We explain the reasoning in plain language — and if we believe the call is wrong, we take it up with the insurer.

We'll Call You

Settlement

The insurer disburses to your account or settles directly with the hospital or garage. We confirm the money has landed before we close the file.

3–5 working days

Timelines are set by your insurer and vary by claim type and document completeness. Our part — intimation, document checks, follow-up — we control
