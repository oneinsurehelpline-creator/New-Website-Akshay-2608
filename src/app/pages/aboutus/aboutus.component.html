Our story

Built on a simple belief:
insurance is human work.

OneInsure was founded with the vision of making insurance more accessible, dependable, and relationship-driven. We've grown through a strong belief that trust is built on the ground — person by person, conversation by conversation.

Our founders built the organisation with a clear focus on creating a company that combines professional expertise with a genuinely human approach to service. Over the years, we've built a strong on-ground presence across markets through teams that believe in personal connections, long-term trust, and genuine service.

Our strength comes from the people behind the organisation — advisors, relationship managers, servicing teams, and leaders who work closely with customers every single day.

On the ground

Real teams. Real conversations. Every day.

Our presence isn't a phone number on a website. It's people in offices, at customer doorsteps, on the phone at 9pm, in hospital corridors during claims.
