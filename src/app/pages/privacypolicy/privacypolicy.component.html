01 / OVERVIEW

The short version.

In plain English
This policy explains what we collect, why, where it goes, and how we keep it safe. If anything's unclear — write to us.

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which OneInsure collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information from users of www.oneinsure.com. It applies to the site and every product and service we offer.

OneInsure is powered by Robinhood Insurance Broker Limited, a licensed insurance broking company registered with the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI · License Code 386).

02 / PERSONAL INFORMATION

What you tell us about yourself.

In plain English
Name, contact, payment info — only what's needed to find you a policy. Skip a field and we'll skip a step. You can also browse without telling us anything.

We collect personal identification details when you register, place an order, subscribe to a newsletter, respond to a survey, fill a form, or use any other resource on our site. This may include your name, email, mailing address, phone number, and payment details.

You can visit the site anonymously. We only collect personal information when you voluntarily provide it — and you can refuse, though that may limit some features.

03 / NON-PERSONAL DATA

Technical stuff we see automatically.

In plain English
Your browser, your OS, your connection type. Anonymous, aggregated, used to make the site faster — never to identify you.

When you interact with our site, we may collect anonymous technical information — your browser name, device type, operating system, internet service provider, and similar connection details. None of this identifies you personally.

04 / COOKIES

The cookie conversation.

In plain English
Small files on your computer that remember your session. Turn them off in your browser if you want — some things may break.

Our site may use cookies to enhance your experience. Your browser places cookies on your device for record-keeping and to remember preferences.

You can change your browser settings to refuse cookies or alert you when they're being sent. Note that some parts of the site may not function properly if you disable them.

05 / HOW WE USE IT

What we do with your information.

In plain English
To find your policy, take your payment, improve the service, and email you stuff you said yes to. That's it.
To improve customer service
Respond to your requests and support needs faster.
To personalize your experience
Understand how people use the site so we can make it better.
To process payments
Only to complete the order you placed. Never shared otherwise.
To send periodic emails
Updates, news, helpful resources — only if you opted in.
To gather feedback
So we can fix what's broken and build what you actually need.
To run promotions or surveys
When you've signed up to hear from us about things we think you'll like.

Every email we send has a one-click unsubscribe at the bottom. You can also email us at any time and we'll take you off the list.

06 / HOW WE PROTECT IT

Security, the way banks do it.

In plain English
Everything sensitive moves over SSL with digital signatures. Nothing critical is ever stored in plaintext.

We adopt strict data-collection, storage, and processing practices to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information, username, password, transaction data, and anything else stored on our site.

SSL-encrypted & digitally signed
Sensitive data exchanged between your browser and our servers travels over an SSL-secured channel — encrypted, signed, and verifiable.

Only with insurers and service partners who help us send you emails, SMS, WhatsApp, or calls — and only with your permission.
