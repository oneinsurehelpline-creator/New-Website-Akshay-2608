How we work

Values we actually live by

Not poster words. These show up in how we give advice, build product, and treat each other every day.

Customer first, always

Every decision starts with one question: is this in the customer's best interest? Not the insurer's, not ours.

Move with urgency

Claims don't wait. Neither should good ideas. We bias for action and ship, learn, and iterate fast.

Radical honesty

We give feedback directly and receive it gracefully. No politics, no filters — just truth in service of growth.

Data over gut

Opinions are welcome. Evidence is required. We run experiments, read numbers, and follow the data wherever it leads.

Own the outcome

No blame game. If something is broken, you have permission — and responsibility — to fix it.

Never stop learning

The insurance landscape is changing. We invest in people who stay curious and turn every setback into a lesson.
