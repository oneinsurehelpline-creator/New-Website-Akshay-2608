Raising a complaint

Include these so we can resolve it faster:

  • Your name and registered mobile number
  • Policy number and insurer name
  • What happened, with dates
  • What outcome you're looking for
  • Copies of any letters, emails or documents

What happens next

  • We acknowledge your complaint and share a reference number.
  • A dedicated officer reviews it and contacts the insurer if needed.
  • You get a written response with the outcome and reasons.
